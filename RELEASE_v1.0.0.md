## Design of permeable pressure tunnels for reinforced concrete linings under internal pressure using Schleiss theory

**Short name:** Schleiss Pressure Tunnel Design  
**Developer:** Mostafa Shahmoradi / مصطفی شاهمرادی

First public release of the online calculator for permeable pressure tunnels and vertical shafts with reinforced concrete linings under internal pressure, based on Schleiss theory (§7.4).

### Features
- Uncracked and cracked phases (Birkenmaier crack series)
- Structure modes: ABOGWL, BELGWL, VSHAFT (+ Auto)
- Detailed per-formula outputs with units and tooltips
- Charts (2a–Pi, 2a–σs2, q–Pi) with series markers
- Reinforcement optimization under designer limits (2a, q, R, σs)
- Multi-layer reinforcement, CSV export, sensitivity analysis
- Bilingual UI (Persian / English) and light / dark themes
- Printable calculation notebook and professional Help manual
- Sample cases validated against ABOGWL / BELGWL / VSHAFT spreadsheets
